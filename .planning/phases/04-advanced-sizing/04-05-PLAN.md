<objective>
Build the statistical validation engine that proves whether Kelly sizing improves PnL compared to Phase 3 dynamic sizing.

Purpose: Without statistical validation, Kelly "looking better" could be random chance. This plan creates a rigorous validation tool that runs paired t-tests and bootstrap confidence intervals across replay sessions. Results must show p < 0.05 AND positive confidence interval to prove improvement. This is the final gatekeeper before trusting Kelly with real money.

Output: Tested KellyValidator that produces paired t-test results, bootstrap confidence intervals, and secondary metrics (capital utilization, profit factor, max loss).
</objective>
